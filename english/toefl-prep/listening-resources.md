## Daily Listening Method

Use this process for 10-20 minutes.

1. Listen once without subtitles.
2. Write 3-5 keywords you heard.
3. Listen again with English subtitles or transcript.
4. Repeat 3-5 sentences aloud.
5. Say a short summary in English.

## Short Summary Template

```text
This video was about ______.
The speaker explained ______.
I understood the main idea, but ______ was difficult.
Next time, I want to listen for ______.
```
